Paste your own text here, then run:

  node data/own/prepare.js && node train.js config/own_brain.js

Anything you paste becomes the brain: its writing style, its knowledge, and —
if you include task transcripts like the example below — its ability to use
tools. Plain writing alone makes a chat-style brain; transcripts make a
task-doing one, so mix in as many as feel natural:

[user] what is 6*7?
[Agent] I can compute that.
act: calc(6*7)
[observed] calc(6*7) -> 42
[Agent] That's 42.

[user] save "hello world" to notes.txt
[Agent] I'll write it.
act: write_file("notes.txt", "hello world")
[observed] write_file -> wrote notes.txt
[Agent] Saved to notes.txt.

Replace the demo lines with examples relevant to what YOUR AI should do —
the more your examples look like real conversations you expect, the more the
trained agent behaves like them.
